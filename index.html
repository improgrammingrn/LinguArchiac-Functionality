THE INSPIRATION

Why LinguArchiac?

Languages carry more than words. They carry stories, traditions, memories, and ways of understanding the world. When a language disappears, much more than vocabulary can be lost with it.

LinguArchiac was created to make endangered languages easier to discover, explore, and preserve through technology.

OUR MISSION

Preserve. Connect. Amplify.

Preserve

Document languages and the stories connected to them for future generations.

Connect

Make endangered languages easier for people to discover and meaningfully explore.

Amplify

Give communities a space to share stories, voices, and cultural knowledge.
