ABOUT LINGUARCHIAC

Preserving languages through stories.

LinguArchiac is an interactive archive designed to help preserve endangered languages and the stories, traditions, and voices connected to them.

Through language exploration, archived stories, audio, translations, and an AI-powered search engine, LinguArchiac brings endangered languages into an accessible digital space.

OUR MISSION

Every language carries a world of knowledge.

LinguArchiac aims to make endangered languages easier to discover, explore, and preserve while keeping the people and stories behind those languages at the center.
